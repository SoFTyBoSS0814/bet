Terms of Service

Last updated: April 2026 (Beta version - v2.1)

1. Registration and Communication

Users are required to provide a valid and functional email address during registration. If the provided email address is unreachable, non-existent, or not owned by the user, we reserve the right to restrict the account. All communication and security verification will be conducted exclusively through this email address.

2. Fraud Prevention and Sanctions

In case of any fraud, abuse, or exploitation of system vulnerabilities, StakeForge will take the following actions:

  • Immediate and permanent account suspension.
  • Indefinite holding of account balance for investigation purposes.
  • Removal of any illegally obtained winnings.

3. Withdrawal Policy and Fees

Withdrawals are processed manually. The system deducts a processing fee from the requested amount, and the remaining "net" amount is sent to the specified BTC address.

Requested Withdrawal Amount Processing Fee
20 € - 100 € 2 €
101 € - 500 € 5 €
501 € - 1000 € 15 €
Above 1000 € 2% of the amount

Example: If you request a 100 € withdrawal, the fee is 2 €, so you will receive 98 € worth of BTC.

* We reserve the right to modify fees in case of extreme BTC network congestion.

4. Beta Phase and P2P Responsibility

The platform is classified as beta software. Markets are created and closed by users themselves. While the Administrator has the right to override clearly erroneous market closures, StakeForge does not assume direct financial responsibility for user-to-user transactions or software errors.
