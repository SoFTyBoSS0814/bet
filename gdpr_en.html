Data Processing Notice (GDPR)

Last updated: April 2026 (v1.1)

1. Principles of Data Processing

The StakeForge system follows the principle of minimal data collection. We only store data that is strictly necessary for the operation of the platform and user identification.

2. Scope of Collected Data

During registration and usage, we collect the following data:

  • Identifiers: Username and email address.
  • Security: Encrypted password (stored as a hash – we never see the raw password).
  • Balances: Current DEMO and REAL balance values.
  • Financial data: Unique BTC wallet address assigned to the account (if eligibility applies).
  • Technical data: IP address and geographic location (for access control verification purposes).

3. Restrictions (Geo-blocking)

The Service Provider applies geo-based restrictions in accordance with applicable laws. Users connecting from Hungarian IP addresses are not allowed to access REAL (real-money) balance funding or BTC wallet generation features. Such users may only use DEMO functionality.

4. Data Security and Third Parties

Your data is stored on the Supabase encrypted cloud platform. We do not sell or share your personal data (email address, username) with third parties. Data is used exclusively for authentication, account management, and service operation.

5. Your Rights

You may request export, modification, or permanent deletion of your data at any time. In case of deletion, all personal data (email, IP, wallet address) will be removed from our system, except transaction logs, which may be retained in anonymized form for statistical and security purposes.

6. Acceptance of Terms

By registering an account and using this service, you explicitly confirm that you have read, fully understood, and unconditionally accepted this policy and all related rules. Your continued use of the service constitutes full agreement to these terms.

The service is provided strictly under these conditions. Any disputes, claims, or complaints will be handled exclusively according to applicable legal regulations and internal platform policies. By using the service, you acknowledge and accept these limitations.
