Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 (v1.2)

This document is the official Privacy Policy of the StakeForge platform. The Data Controller hereby informs the platform users (hereinafter: Data Subject or User) about the order, terms, and guarantees of personal data processing, in accordance with the Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter: GDPR) and applicable national legislation.

I. IDENTITY AND CONTACT DETAILS OF THE DATA CONTROLLER [GDPR Art. 13 (1)(a)]

The operator of the Platform acts as the Data Controller regarding the personal data of the Users.

Name of Data Controller: Individual Development Project | StakeForge

Official Data Protection Contact (e-mail): support@stake-forge.com

II. PRINCIPLES OF DATA PROCESSING [GDPR Art. 5]

The Data Controller declares that personal data processing is performed strictly in accordance with the principles of lawfulness, fairness and transparency, purpose limitation, data minimization, accuracy, storage limitation, as well as integrity and confidentiality.

III. SCOPE, PURPOSE, LEGAL BASIS AND DURATION OF DATA PROCESSING [GDPR Art. 13 (1)(c)]

The Data Controller performs data processing based on the principle of minimization, categorized as follows:

1. Registration and Identification Data

Data processed: System-generated unique identifier (UUID), Username, E-mail address.
Purpose: Authentication of the Data Subject, creation of the user account (registration), provision of the service covered by the Agreement, and official communication.
Legal basis: Performance of a contract [GDPR Art. 6 (1)(b)].
Retention period: For the duration of the user relationship (until the deletion of the registration).

2. Security and Authentication Data

Data processed: Authentication tokens (e.g., OAuth ID, session identifier). The Platform does not store passwords; authentication is carried out through secure third-party providers.
Purpose: Ensuring the integrity of the user account and protection against unauthorized access.
Legal basis: Compliance with a legal obligation (ensuring data security) [GDPR Art. 6 (1)(c), with regard to Art. 32].
Retention period: For the duration of the user relationship.

3. Financial and Asset-Related Data

Data processed: Current state of the User's DEMO (virtual) balance, REAL (actual) balance status, unique BTC (Bitcoin) wallet address.
Purpose: Technical and accounting processing of economic events (bets, winnings) and deposit/withdrawal transactions within the Platform.
Legal basis: Performance of a contract [GDPR Art. 6 (1)(b)].
Retention period: Until the deletion of the user account, subject to mandatory limitation and retention periods prescribed by applicable accounting and tax laws.

4. Transaction and Betting Log Data

Data processed: Transaction/bet unique identifier (UUID), Market ID, User unique identifier (User UUID), Outcome description, Applied odds, Bet amount, Transaction type, Transaction legal status.
Purpose: Settlement of the technical operation of the service, legal protection of system integrity, and prevention and detection of fraudulent behavior.
Legal basis: Performance of a contract [GDPR Art. 6 (1)(b)], and the Data Controller’s legitimate interest in maintaining system security [GDPR Art. 6 (1)(f)].
Retention period: In case of account deletion, the personal link (User UUID) is immediately and irreversibly severed. Following the termination of the relationship, the system continues to store this data solely in an aggregated, anonymized form, which qualifies as statistical data outside the scope of the GDPR.

5. Network and Technical Data

Data processed: The network IP address of the User's terminal device.
Purpose: Real-time execution of geo-based eligibility checks (Geo-blocking) and ensuring compliance with relevant laws.
Legal basis: Compliance with a legal obligation (compliance with international gambling and anti-money laundering laws) [GDPR Art. 6 (1)(c)].
Retention period: Data is not stored in the database. Processing of the IP address is limited solely to real-time analysis of network traffic in memory.

IV. LEGAL DISCLAIMER REGARDING TERRITORIAL RESTRICTIONS (GEO-BLOCKING)

Due to compliance with international and national regulatory requirements, the Data Controller employs strict network-based filtering. For Users connecting from Hungarian IP addresses, the Website exclusively provides access to DEMO (virtual, non-monetary value) features. For this category of users, the activation of the REAL (actual financial) balance and the generation of BTC wallet addresses are blocked at a technical level.

The Data Controller explicitly states that any conduct aimed at bypassing the above technical restriction (including, but not limited to, the use of virtual private networks – VPNs – or proxy servers) constitutes a material, unilateral breach of the Terms and Conditions, which results in the immediate termination of the user account without liability for damages.

V. INTERNATIONAL DATA TRANSFER AND PROCESSORS [GDPR Art. 28 and 46]

To ensure the IT infrastructure of the Platform, the Data Controller utilizes Supabase Inc. (address: 1355 Market St, Suite 488, San Francisco, CA 94103, USA) as a cloud database service processor. Given the processor's location, the legality of data transfer is guaranteed by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, thereby fulfilling the high-level data protection requirements set forth in Art. 46 of the GDPR.

The Data Controller declares that it does not transfer personal data to third parties for marketing or market research purposes, whether free of charge or for consideration.

VI. DATA SECURITY AND TECHNICAL PROTECTION [GDPR Art. 32]

The Data Controller undertakes to ensure data security through technical and organizational measures corresponding to the state of the art. Network communication is conducted exclusively via encrypted (TLS/SSL) channels. Database access is controlled by Row Level Security (RLS) policies, which provide a technical barrier against unauthorized access to data.

VII. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECTS AND THEIR EXERCISE [GDPR Art. 15-22]

Based on the provisions of the GDPR, the Data Subject may exercise the following rights by sending a declaration to the e-mail address support@stake-forge.com:

  • Right to information and access (Art. 15): The Data Subject has the right to receive feedback on the data processing process and to request a copy of the personal data being processed.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): The Data Subject has the right to request the correction of inaccurate data or the completion of incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") (Art. 17): The Data Subject has the right to initiate the permanent deletion of their account and all personal data.
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18): In the event of a legal dispute or suspicion of unlawful data processing, the Data Subject may request the suspension of data processing operations.
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20): The Data Subject has the right to receive the data provided by them in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON) or to have it transmitted to another controller.
  • Right to object (Art. 21): The Data Subject has the right to object to the processing of their personal data based on legitimate interest [Art. 6 (1)(f)].

Explicit warning regarding the right to object: The technical, financial, and accounting system of the Platform forms an inseparable unity. If the Data Subject exercises their right to object by prohibiting the processing of transaction and security log data, it will result in the technical and legal impossibility of providing the Service. Therefore, the Data Controller informs the Data Subject that exercising the right to object results in the immediate, permanent deletion of the User registration (account).

The Data Controller shall evaluate and fulfill incoming requests without undue delay, within one month (30 days) of receipt, free of charge.

VIII. LEGAL REMEDIES [GDPR Art. 77 and 79]

If, in the opinion of the Data Subject, the Data Controller's activity violates data protection laws, they may exercise the following legal enforcement options:

  • Consultation with the Data Controller: It is recommended to first report the suspected infringement to the Data Controller at support@stake-forge.com for a peaceful and quick resolution.
  • Initiation of official proceedings (Art. 77): The Data Subject has the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.
  • Judicial enforcement (Art. 79): Independent of any administrative proceedings, the Data Subject has the right to turn to a court before the tribunal competent for the Data Controller's seat or the Data Subject's own domestic place of residence (domicile).
